Our latest research has been published in *Science Advances*. This collaborative work investigates the mutational landscape and functional effects of noncoding ultraconserved elements in human cancers, providing new insights into how these highly conserved genomic regions contribute to cancer biology. The study represents a large-scale, multi-institutional effort advancing our understanding of non-coding genome function in oncology.
